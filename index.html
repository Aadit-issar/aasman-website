Aasman (आसमान)

A private personal AI assistant that one person uses, through WhatsApp, to manage reminders, tasks, study plans and their own Google account.

Aasman is not a public service. It has no sign-up, no other users and no accounts. It is built and used by a single person, its owner, who is the only person who can use it and the only person whose Google data it can reach.

Aasman is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by Google. Google, Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets and Google Contacts are trademarks of Google LLC.

What Aasman does

  • Takes requests from its owner over WhatsApp, including typed messages, images, voice notes and PDF or document attachments.
  • Creates and delivers reminders and recurring task summaries on a schedule kept on the owner's own device.
  • Helps with study plans, practice tracking and document or transcript reading for the owner.
  • Searches the public web for the owner and reads public pages.
  • Works with the owner's Google account, only when the owner asks, as described below.

Google integrations

The owner connects their own Google account once, through Google's standard OAuth consent screen. Aasman then uses Google's APIs only in response to a request the owner sends it. It does not watch the mailbox or calendar in the background.

Google Calendar

List events, check free/busy time, and create, update or delete events on the owner's primary calendar. Deleting needs explicit confirmation.

Gmail

Search and read messages, label them, save drafts, and send an email only to the owner's own address after an explicit request. Aasman cannot send to anyone else.

Google Drive

Search and read files (Docs and Sheets are read as text or CSV) and create new plain-text files. Aasman has no delete or overwrite action for Drive files.

Google Docs

Create documents, read them, append text and find-and-replace text on request.

Google Sheets

Create spreadsheets, read ranges and write cell values on request.

Google Contacts

Look up the owner's saved contacts by name, email or phone. Read-only.

Details of what is accessed, where it is stored and how to revoke access are in the Privacy Policy. The rules for using the project are in the Terms of Service.
